### Key Statutory Provisions:

| Regulation / Authority | Specific Clause / Circular | What the Law Actually Says |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RBI KYC Master Direction** | **Section 16 (Offline Verification)** | Regulated Entities (Banks, NBFCs) are explicitly authorized to use **Digitally Signed Aadhaar XML / Secure QR** for Customer Due Diligence (CDD). |
| **RBI KYC Master Direction** | **Section 18 (V-CIP Amendment)** | Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) was officially notified on Jan 9, 2020. RBI explicitly states: **"V-CIP shall be treated on par with In-Person Verification (IPV)."** |
| **SEBI KYC Guidelines** | **Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOP/CIR/P/2020/73** | SEBI allowed **VIPV (Video In-Person Verification)**, App-based KYC, and DigiLocker / Aadhaar XML, officially waiving physical presence for stock broking and mutual funds. |
| **PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act)** | **Rule 9 (Amended 2019 & 2023)** | Reporting entities can verify client identity via **Offline Verification under Aadhaar Act** or electronic records from DigiLocker. Physical presence is NOT mandated. |
| **Aadhaar Act 2019** | **Section 4(4) & Section 8A** | Codified "Offline Verification" without centralized biometric matching, giving citizens the statutory right to share digitally signed proofs without giving physical paper copies. |
| **DPDP Act 2023** | **Section 6(1) & Section 33** | Mandates **Data Minimization**. Storing raw photocopies, unredacted 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, or unencrypted customer files exposes firms to **₹250 Crore fines**. |
