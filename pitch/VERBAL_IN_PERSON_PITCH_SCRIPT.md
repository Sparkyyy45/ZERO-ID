*(Dismantle the incumbents before he can ask about them.)*

That directly violates Section 6 of the DPDP Act, which legally mandates Data Minimization.

### 2. "Why won't banks just stick to Video KYC?"
> *"Because Video KYC costs ₹25 to ₹35 per call, requires scheduling human agents, and has a 45% drop-off rate because users hate sitting on a live video call. We do the exact same verification in 1.5 seconds for ₹3. Every fintech CFO wants to cut that bill."*

### 3. "Is this legally valid under RBI rules?"
> *"Yes, 100%. Section 16 of the RBI KYC Master Direction explicitly recognizes Offline Verification of digitally signed Aadhaar as a valid Customer Due Diligence method for bank accounts and credit lines. We are 100% compliant with RBI and DPDP."*

### 4. "How do you get your first 10 customers? (CAC)"
> *"We target mid-tier digital lending apps and NBFCs who disburse small personal loans. Their sales cycle is 4 to 6 weeks. We give them a 2-line React SDK with the first 1,000 checks free. They integrate it in an afternoon, see their drop-off reduce, and switch their traffic over."*

### 5. "What if a fraudster steals someone's Aadhaar file?"
> *"They can't use it. Every proof is cryptographically bound to the physical device's Secure Enclave using FIDO2 WebAuthn. Presenting a proof requires a live physical fingerprint or FaceID. Without the victim's physical thumb, the stolen file is worthless."*
